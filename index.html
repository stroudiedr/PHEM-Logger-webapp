Features

Built for the timeline of a real job

From activation to handover, PHEM Logger captures the moments that matter for clinical governance, handover documentation and post-incident audit.

01 JOB TIMINGS

Every phase, to the second

Activation, mobile, on scene, at patient, leaving scene and hospital arrival, with a live on-scene stopwatch. Time since call, scene time, patient contact and total job time are worked out for you.

02 DRUGS

Doses, with counts

Log clinician-administered drugs, including adrenaline (bolus and infusion), fentanyl, ketamine, rocuronium, TXA and morphine. Repeat doses are counted automatically. Record now, at another time, or with no time.

03 INTERVENTIONS

Precise procedure times

Airway management, PHEA, blood products, IV and arterial access, ultrasound and fracture management, each with an exact timestamp.

04 CARDIAC ARREST

Arrest events and intervals

Shocks, rhythm changes, ROSC, mechanical CPR, arrest drugs and termination, plus initial events such as bystander CPR and AED. No-flow, low-flow and total down times are calculated automatically.

05 NEWS2

Scored from your observations

Record a vital signs snapshot and the NEWS2 score is calculated and colour-coded, with a Scale 2 option. A score only appears once all seven parameters are in; anything missing is named.

06 ATMIST HANDOVER

Notes that map to handover

Structure your notes your way and map them straight onto ATMIST. The handover then reads as plain text at the receiving hospital.

07 EXPORT & PRINT

Share it your way

Export as plain text, a spreadsheet, or an archive covering several jobs. You can also print to Bixolon SPP-R310 or Zebra ZQ220 Plus thermal printers, with a printer check at the start of each shift.

08 JOB LIST

Outcomes at a glance

Each completed job shows how it ended: handover, stand down or life extinct. Filter by outcome, then export or delete several jobs at once.

09 GUIDE & SIMULATION

Ready for training too

A built-in sixteen-section guide covers every screen, including data governance. The app works just as well in manikin simulation for debriefs and skills development.

Export & print

From the job to the handover

When the job is done, the record needs to go somewhere: to the receiving team, your notes or a governance review. PHEM Logger gives you several ways to get it there, and it only leaves your device when you choose.

EXPORT & SHARE

Share a job in the format you need

You can view and share a timings report during a job or at the end of it. You can also export from the job list.

  • Plain-text handover. A clean preview that's easy to read aloud or show at handover.
  • Spreadsheet. Timings, drugs, interventions and observations in rows and columns for audit and review.
  • Multi-job archive. Select several jobs and export them together in one archive, including a data backup file.
  • Send anywhere. Use the share sheet to send to Notes, email or a messaging app.
  • Everything included. Exports include NEWS2 scores, cardiac arrest intervals and your ATMIST notes.

THERMAL PRINTING

A printed record for the receiving team

Print a handover slip to a portable Bluetooth thermal printer, so the receiving team has the timings on paper before you leave.

  • Supported printers. Bixolon SPP-R310 and Zebra ZQ220 Plus.
  • Saved printers. Keep a list of the printers your service uses and pick one quickly.
  • Start-of-shift check. Confirm paper, battery and connection before you need them.

On your wrist, in your pocket, on the tablet

Privacy

Your data never leaves your device

PHEM Logger has no servers, no accounts and no analytics. Everything you record stays on your device unless you choose to export, share or print it.

Read the full privacy policy →

  • No accounts. There's nothing to sign up for or log into.
  • No analytics or tracking. Your use of the app is never measured or reported.
  • No internet needed. Every feature works offline.
  • No patient identifiers needed. The app never asks for names, NHS numbers or dates of birth.
  • You choose what's shared. Data only leaves the device when you export, share or print it.
